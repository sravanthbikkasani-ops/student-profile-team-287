About the college

Founded in 1991 by a group of local teachers, Lakeview has grown from one building and 120 students into a full campus, while keeping classes small.

Our mission

To give every student the skills, confidence and habits they need to build a career and contribute to their community.

We measure ourselves by what graduates go on to do, not by rankings.

Campus

Forty acres by the lake: seven academic blocks, a central library, sports ground, hostels for 900 students and a 24-hour study hall.

Our story

  1. 1991
    First batch of 120 students begins in a single block.
  2. 2004
    Engineering and science departments open.
  3. 2013
    Central library and innovation lab built.
  4. 2022
    Business school and placement cell launch.
